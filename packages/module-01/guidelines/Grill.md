# Grill

A row of stadium slots like a pegboard or speaker grill.

Optional `count` (default 6), `slotWidth`, `gap`, `tone` (`recess` default, `signal` for orange slots on shell, `on-signal` for slots cut into a signal surface).

- Slots are `radius-full`, sunken with `shadow-press`. Use as a texture on the hero side, the call-to-action block and card corners, a few slots at a time.
