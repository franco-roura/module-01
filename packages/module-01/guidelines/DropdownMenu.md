# DropdownMenu

A menu of actions that opens from a button, on Radix DropdownMenu.

Compose `DropdownMenu`, `DropdownMenuTrigger` and `DropdownMenuContent` with `DropdownMenuItem`s. Labels, separators, groups, checkbox and radio items and submenus are available. `variant="destructive"` marks the deleting action.

- Items are verbs in sentence case ("Rename", "Move to…"), with an optional Lucide glyph before the label.
- Put the destructive item last, after a separator.
- For actions. Choosing a value is a Select.
