# GlassPanel

Frosted clear-plastic panel, like the UFO dome or a clear case, that lets what is behind it show through.

Provide `children`. Use over something with color or texture (a studio image, the signal block, a grill); on plain canvas it disappears.

- `glass` fill, 16px backdrop blur, `shadow-glass` edge highlight, `radius-lg`.
- At most one or two per page beyond the nav bar. Text on it stays `ink`.
