# IconPicker and ColorPicker

Swatch grids for choosing a category's glyph and palette color.

Both take `value` and `onChange`. `IconPicker` offers the curated Lucide set; `ColorPicker` offers the sixteen `cat-*` colors.

- They're native radio groups under the swatches, so arrow keys move the choice.
- Show a CategoryIcon preview next to them so people see the pair together.
