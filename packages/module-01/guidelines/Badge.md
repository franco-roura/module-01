# Badge

Stadium chip in HUD type for states and counts.

Provide `children` (one or two words). Optional `variant`.

- `default`: `shell-recess` fill, like a slot molded into the part.
- `signal`: the one badge that matters most. `tint`: in-progress states. `outline`: quiet, on shell.
- `secondary`: a raised `shell` chip with `shadow-mold`, for use on `canvas` or `shell-recess`.
- `destructive`: `danger` text on `danger-tint`, for errors.
- Text is always uppercase `hud`. Keep it short; a sentence belongs in body text.
