# CompareList

Before and after, line by line: what people do now, crossed out, and what they get instead, checked. Common on landing pages.

Provide `items` (`[{instead, get}]`). Optional `insteadLabel` ("Instead of") and `getLabel` ("You get"), read aloud before each line, and `showLabels` to print them as microprint too.

- The old way sits next to a recessed `shell-recess` cross in `ink-soft`; the new way next to a `signal` check, in semibold `ink`.
- Icons are Lucide X and Check at the kit's 1.25 stroke. Each line carries its label for screen readers, so the icons are never the only cue.
- Keep each line to one short sentence, and two to four items per list.
