# SectionHeader

Section opener: HUD label with code, a plain display heading, and an optional tick ruler.

Provide `title` (plain words: "Overview", "Settings"). Optional `code` (`S-02`), `label` (overline text), `aside` (a button or link at the right), `ruler`, and `as` (`h1` default, `h2`, `h3`).

- Heading in `display-lg` (Michroma), `ink`; `display-md` on phones. Copy stays plain; the codes and ticks carry the sci-fi.
- Leave `space-12` between the header and the content of the section.
- One `h1` per page; use `as` for the sections under it.
