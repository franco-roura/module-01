# Tooltip

A short `ink` label that names a control on hover and focus, on Radix Tooltip.

Wrap the app once in `TooltipProvider`, then compose `Tooltip`, `TooltipTrigger` and `TooltipContent`.

- For icon buttons and truncated text. One short phrase, no punctuation.
- Never put information there that people need to finish a task: touch screens have no hover.
