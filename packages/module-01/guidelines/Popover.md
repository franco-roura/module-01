# Popover

A floating `shell` part anchored to a trigger, on Radix Popover.

Compose `Popover`, `PopoverTrigger` and `PopoverContent` (`align`, `side`, `sideOffset`). `PopoverHeader`, `PopoverTitle` and `PopoverDescription` give it a heading.

- `shell` fill, `radius-lg`, `shadow-float`.
- For pickers and small forms. Use the kit's pickers (Combobox, DatePicker) before building one by hand.
