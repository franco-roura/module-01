# Sheet

A panel that slides in from an edge, for editors and filters. Built on Radix Dialog.

Compose `Sheet`, `SheetTrigger` and `SheetContent` (`side`: `right` default, `left`, `top`, `bottom`) with a `SheetHeader`, your form, and a `SheetFooter`.

- From the right on desktop at `sheet-max` (560px) wide; from the bottom on phones.
- Like Dialog, it opens on its first field and keeps focus inside.
- The footer holds the main action; keep it visible while the body scrolls.
