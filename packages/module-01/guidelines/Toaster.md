# Toaster

Toasts on sonner, in Module/01 colors.

Mount `<Toaster />` once at the root, then call `toast()`, `toast.success()` or `toast.error()` from `sonner`.

- A toast confirms what just happened ("Changes saved") or reports a problem. One short sentence, no period.
- When a form field is rejected, fault the field as well (see Field); the toast carries the message.
- Never put the only copy of important information in a toast: it leaves.
