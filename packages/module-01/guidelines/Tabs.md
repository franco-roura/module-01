# Tabs

A row of stadium tabs that switch the view under them, on Radix Tabs.

Compose `Tabs` (`value` or `defaultValue`) with a `TabsList` of `TabsTrigger`s and one `TabsContent` per tab. `TabsList` takes `variant` `default` or `line`.

- The list is a sunken `shell-recess` track; the active tab is a raised `shell` stadium with `shadow-mold`.
- Two to five tabs with one-word labels. More than that is a Select.
- In a form, tabs that switch its kind ("Expense", "Income", "Transfer") sit above the first field.
