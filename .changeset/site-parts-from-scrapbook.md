Add `Accordion`, `Message` (with `Bubble`) and `ShareBar` to the kit, and `CompareList` to the site entry. `NavBar` takes `mobileMenu`, which folds its links into a sheet on phones, and `Tag` takes `wrap` for short taglines that would otherwise overflow.

`CtaBanner` takes `children`, set full width under the text for a signup form, with fields restyled to read on signal. `Hero` takes `imageShape="wide"` (and `HeroFrame` a `shape`) for a 16:10 screenshot frame.
